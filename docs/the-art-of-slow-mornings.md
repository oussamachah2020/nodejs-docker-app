# The Art of Slow Mornings

*June 15, 2026*

---

There's a quiet kind of rebellion in refusing to rush.

In a world that rewards the earliest email, the fastest reply, the most packed schedule — choosing a slow morning is a small act of defiance. It says: *I belong to myself first.*

## What a slow morning looks like

- Coffee made without checking your phone
- A window watched for a few minutes — clouds, light, the neighbor's cat
- Thoughts allowed to arrive on their own, without being summoned by a notification
- Breakfast eaten at a table, not over a sink

## Why it matters

The brain doesn't switch on like a lamp. It warms up, like an old engine on a cold day. When we force it into high gear the moment we open our eyes, we skip the quiet window where our best ideas live — that half-awake state where problems solve themselves and creativity flows without effort.

Psychologists call it the **hypnopompic state**. Artists call it *the golden hour of the mind*.

## A small practice

Tomorrow morning, before you reach for your phone:

1. Sit up slowly
2. Take three deep breaths
3. Look out a window for sixty seconds
4. *Then* begin your day

You won't lose anything. You might gain everything.
